## Format notes

- **`.webp`** — the site serves AVIF/WebP and every existing image is `.webp`. Convert
  a generated PNG/JPG before saving, or the path will not match.
- **Product shots** are displayed uncropped on a cream background, so a plain or
  transparent backdrop works best. Existing product photos are the reference.
- **Category covers** are square 1:1 collages, visually distinct from product shots.
- **Filenames must match exactly**, including the `-beaumont-tx` suffix. A mismatch is
  now silently hidden rather than rendered broken, so double-check the spelling.
