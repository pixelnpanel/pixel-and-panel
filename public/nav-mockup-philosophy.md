# Interface Signal

A movement in which interface state becomes visible art — where the act of marking "you are here" 
is treated with the same craft as any considered typographic gesture. The visual vocabulary is spare: 
a line, a shape, a shift in hue. Each signal carries weight through its restraint.

## Form and Space

Navigation exists in a band of concentrated attention. Every mark within it must earn its place. 
Active states communicate not through decoration but through precision — a 3-pixel underline is 
a statement, not an afterthought. Space between elements is as intentional as the elements themselves, 
crafted with painstaking care so that the eye finds its anchor without effort.

## Color and Material

Gold against dark navy is the language of this system. The active signal borrows from the brand's 
own warmth — not imposed, but surfaced. Inactive links recede into cool gray-white, creating a 
natural hierarchy that rewards focused attention. The palette is of the material itself, not applied to it.

## Scale and Rhythm

The navigation bar is a horizon line — elements sit at a consistent baseline, and any departure 
from that rhythm (a pill shape floating, a dot punctuating silence) reads as intentional signal. 
Each option's active treatment respects the horizontal flow while introducing just enough 
disruption to orient the viewer.

## Composition and Balance

Four options. Four distinct philosophies of where to place emphasis: below, around, within, beneath. 
Each is meticulous in its own logic. The underline grounds; the pill encloses; the color transforms; 
the dot whispers. Master-level execution means these distinctions are immediately legible at a glance.
